Test tips

  • Hold the phone upright in the arm mount and use the back camera.
  • Start recording, start the spin, and stop after one or two turns. Up to 12 seconds is used.
  • Keep this screen open while it works. Locking the phone pauses it.
  • On iPhone, tap Save, then choose “Save Video” or “Save Image” to put it in Photos.
  • With the Mustangs ’96 border, the video shows only in the middle box, so keep the whole person in the center of the shot. Step the camera back a little or zoom out to 0.5×.
  • For another border, upload a vertical PNG with a see-through middle. The video is fitted into that opening.
